About me

Do you need someone who can help a growing team become more efficient, run your calendar, protect your confidential records, automate repetitive work, and save your team time and increase revenue? That’s what I do.

For more than 8 years, I’ve been trusted with sensitive data, calendars, correspondence, records, and day-to-day administrative operations across Government and University offices.

Today, I work as a Data Processing Specialist and Confidential Secretary in the Office of the State Auditor-General, Akwa Ibom State Government. I manage the Auditor-General’s calendar and inbox, draft official memos and correspondence, and support the preparation of the Annual Accountability Report and State Financial Report.

But what sets me apart is this: I’m not just processing data, I automate the workflow. I’m an AI automation specialist. In my office, I built an automated workflow integrating Google Drive with WhatsApp, so the moment a document is shared, the team receives a WhatsApp notification automatically and nothing is missed. I also build 24/7 AI agents that answer customer enquiries and capture leads.

I combine my background in administrative operations, executive support, and AI automation to help teams reduce repetitive work, minimize errors, and save valuable time.

I don’t just build and disappear. I test the automation, deploy it properly, make sure it works as expected, and hand it over to you or your team. If you need ongoing support, I can also help with maintenance, troubleshooting, and unexpected errors. Because apps get updated, APIs change, credentials expire, and integrations can break, I can help maintain your automations and keep your workflows running smoothly.

I also manage social media and online communities. I plan and post content, reply to comments and messages, and create posts that get people talking and engaging. I can turn a silent group into an active community. I also set up automated email reminders for meetings, and I pair all of this with automation, so I can schedule your content, welcome new members, and organise enquiries without extra manual work.
